How MealSync Works

A synchronous solution built specifically for hostel students and dining staff.

📋

Live Menus & Veg/Non-Veg Tagging

Check what food is being served at every counter (Pure Veg or Non-Veg) before ever stepping into the mess hall.

🔄

Automatic Line Switching

Students can switch queues at any time. Joining a new line automatically exits the old one, preventing double-queueing.

⏱️

Dynamic Wait Calculations

Powered by the JavaScript time module: tracks exact serving durations and computes moving average speeds for real-time ETAs.

🍽️

Staff Queue Management

Mess staff can create new counters with custom dishes and dequeue students one-by-one with live timer feedback.
